Who We Are

We turn complex genomics into simple solutions.

We’re a genomics research and diagnostics company, a subsidiary of Reliance Industries Limited. We began our journey as a bioinformatics start-up within the innovation ecosystem of the Indian Institute of Science (IISc) and were an early example of academic entrepreneurship in India.

For the past 25 years, we’ve been tackling some of the toughest challenges in healthcare genomics. We transform complex genomic data into actionable insights, providing solutions in clinical diagnostics, translational medicine, bioinformatics, engineering, and science. By collaborating with global partners, we’re helping shape the future of personalized medicine.

Why Genetic Testing Matters

Genetic testing helps us understand the blueprint of life to drive innovations in diagnosis and health.

Rare & Inherited Conditions

Over 70 million people in India suffer from rare and inherited conditions. Early genetic testing can lead to faster diagnosis and treatment.

Carrier Screening

Carrier screening can prevent inherited conditions in children. Testing before or during pregnancy helps couples make informed decisions.

Early Diagnosis = Better Outcomes

Delays in rare disease diagnosis average 7 years. Genetic testing can shorten this "Diagnostic Odyssey" and reduce long-term healthcare costs.

Targeted Therapies

Genetic testing can guide targeted therapies—especially in Breast, Ovarian, Lung, and Colorectal cancers—improving survival.
