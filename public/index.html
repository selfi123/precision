Why Precision Allele.Dx

What sets us apart in an increasingly crowded diagnostics landscape.

Allele-Resolution Technology

Our proprietary bioinformatics pipeline resolves variants at the allele level — detecting low-frequency mutations that conventional assays routinely miss, with a sensitivity threshold below 0.1% VAF.

48-Hour Turnaround

Critical oncology reports delivered in under 48 hours. Our automated wet-lab workflows and AI-assisted interpretation pipelines eliminate bottlenecks without sacrificing clinical accuracy.

AI-Powered Interpretation

Every report is augmented by our clinical AI engine, which cross-references findings against curated global oncology databases, clinical trial registries, and FDA-approved therapy guidelines.

Regulatory Compliance

All diagnostics adhere to NABL, CAP, and ISO 15189 standards. Our processes undergo continuous external quality assessment — ensuring every report meets the highest international benchmarks.

Dedicated Clinical Team

Each partner account is supported by a dedicated medical liaison — a qualified molecular pathologist or genetic counselor available for report walk-throughs, second opinions, and ongoing consultation.

Seamless Digital Integration

Our open API and FHIR-compliant data layer integrates with any existing EMR, HIS, or LIS in days, not months — enabling instant digital transformation of your diagnostic infrastructure.
