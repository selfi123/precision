Clinical Overview

Essential for staging and treatment planning in metastatic colorectal cancer (mCRC), this panel interrogates all-RAS codons, BRAF V600E, HER2 amplifications, and microsatellite instability to inform systemic therapy selection according to NCCN guidelines.

Clinical Utility & Therapeutic Value

  • Determines all-RAS (KRAS/NRAS) wild-type status for anti-EGFR therapy eligibility.
  • Identifies BRAF V600E for targeted encorafenib + cetuximab therapy.
  • Screens for HER2/neu amplification for dual-HER2 blockade.
  • Assesses Microsatellite Instability (MSI-H) for immune checkpoint inhibitor benefit.
