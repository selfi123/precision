Clinical Overview

The Somatic Endometrium Panel evaluates 51 HRR pathway genes and integrates the four molecular subgroups defined by the WHO and ProMisE classifications (POLE ultramutated, MMR-deficient, p53 abnormal, and NSMP). It provides essential prognostic information and guides adjuvant radiation, chemotherapy, and immunotherapy decisions.

Clinical Utility & Therapeutic Value

  • Complete molecular classification aligned with FIGO 2023 staging guidelines.
  • Identifies POLE-mutant patients with favorable outcomes eligible for therapy de-escalation.
  • Detects MMR deficiency predicting high benefit from PD-1 checkpoint inhibitors (pembrolizumab, dostarlimab).
  • Evaluates 51 HRR genes for potential PARP inhibitor sensitivity.
