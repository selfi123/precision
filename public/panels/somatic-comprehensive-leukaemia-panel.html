Clinical Overview

Acute lymphoblastic leukemia (ALL) and acute myeloid leukemia (AML) exhibit complex cytogenetic and molecular heterogeneity. This comprehensive panel interrogates 98 DNA genes and 119 RNA fusion partners to provide complete molecular workup, risk stratification, and targeted therapy selection (e.g. IDH1/2 inhibitors, FLT3 inhibitors, menin inhibitors).

Clinical Utility & Therapeutic Value

  • Comprehensive detection of 119 leukemic RNA fusion transcripts (KMT2A, BCR-ABL1, PML-RARA, CBFB-MYH11).
  • Full sequencing of 98 hematologic driver genes including IDH1, IDH2, FLT3, TP53, RUNX1.
  • Informs ELN risk stratification and minimal residual disease (MRD) baseline marker identification.
  • Guides targeted therapy selection with IDH inhibitors (ivosidenib, enasidenib) and FLT3 inhibitors.

Applicable Cancers & Indications

This diagnostic panel is clinically indicated for the following disease entities:
