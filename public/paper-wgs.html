Clinical Integration of Whole-Genome Sequencing in Solid Cancers

Journal of Precision Oncology • Published: Sept 2026

Abstract

Whole-genome sequencing (WGS) provides a comprehensive view of the cancer genome, yet its routine clinical implementation has been challenging. This study demonstrates the successful integration of WGS into the clinical workflow for patients with advanced solid tumors.

Introduction

Precision oncology relies heavily on identifying actionable genetic variants. While targeted panels have been the standard of care, they often miss complex structural variants and mutational signatures. WGS offers a complete molecular profile, enabling better therapy matching and clinical trial enrollment.

Methods

We analyzed tumor and matched normal samples from 1,500 patients using a high-throughput WGS pipeline. Our proprietary bioinformatics platform was utilized to standardize reporting and reduce turnaround time to under 14 days.

Results

Actionable mutations were identified in 82% of patients, representing a 15% increase over traditional panel testing. Furthermore, complex biomarkers such as Tumor Mutational Burden (TMB) and Homologous Recombination Deficiency (HRD) were calculated with higher accuracy.
