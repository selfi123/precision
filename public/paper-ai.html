Multi-Modal Artificial Intelligence Integration in Precision Oncology

Frontiers in AI • Published: August 2026

Abstract

The rapid expansion of genomic data alongside medical imaging and electronic health records (EHR) presents a unique opportunity for multi-modal AI architectures. We present a deep learning framework capable of fusing genomic profiles with histopathology slides to improve patient stratification.

Introduction

Cancer is a complex, multi-dimensional disease. Analyzing a single data modality, such as DNA sequences alone, often provides an incomplete picture of the tumor microenvironment. Multi-modal AI aims to synthesize insights across various data types, mirroring the holistic approach of a multi-disciplinary tumor board.

Methods

We trained a late-fusion neural network using 10,000 paired whole-exome sequencing (WES) files and H&E stained whole-slide images (WSIs). The genomic branch utilized a Transformer architecture for variant prioritization, while the imaging branch utilized a ResNet-50 backbone.

Results

The multi-modal model achieved a 94% accuracy in predicting response to immunotherapy, outperforming both the genomic-only (86%) and imaging-only (81%) baseline models. These results underscore the importance of data integration in modern bioinformatics pipelines.
